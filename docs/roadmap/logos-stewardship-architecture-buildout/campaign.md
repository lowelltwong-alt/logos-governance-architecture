# Campaign: Logos Stewardship Architecture Buildout

## Outcome

Produce a reviewable architecture-buildout specification that tells future
workers what to research, what artifacts to propose, which experts must review
which questions, how evidence moves through a typed graph, and exactly where
work must stop for a human. The current terminal outcome is a validated
specification and handoff—not implemented architecture.

Completion evidence requires all specification files to parse, the source lock
to replay, the exact 7/2/10/5/SPECIAL boundary to remain unchanged, the campaign
DAG and role graph to be acyclic, writer/checker separation to hold, every
material conclusion to reverse-traverse to source evidence, and an independent
read-only review to report no false authority.

No edge from advisory consensus leads directly to implementation. Block edges
dominate readiness edges. `SPECIAL-001` stays `UNKNOWN`; neither a numeric
parent limit nor an experiment is authorized.

## Mesh shape

The current planning mesh has one repository writer, one independent checker,
read-only synthesis foremen, lateral specialists, and separately named
human-only authority roles. Foremen do not dispatch or delegate. A later
qualified controller would assign every role directly, activate at most three
lanes at once, and remain the only owner of leases, state transitions, budget
accounting, or append-only runtime receipts. Specialists produce bounded
evidence packets; they do not write repository artifacts or approve their own
work.

Research lanes are activated only for distinct questions:

- primary-source identity, quotation fitness, original-language escalation, and exegesis boundaries;
- historical theology, genealogy, tradition scope, and institution-authority separation;
- knowledge-graph, provenance, retrieval, source lineage, and reverse-consumer engineering;
- empirical methods, statistics, assurance independence, and route evaluation;
- privacy, security, custody, authentication, retention, and publication risk;
- rights, licensing, access, and unavailable-source dockets;
- migration safety, compatibility, rollback, and kill criteria;
- maintainer workflow, accessibility, and loss-bounded projections.

Qualified human experts—not agent labels—own doctrine, canonicity, exegesis,
tradition policy, privacy/custody acceptance, and final architecture decisions.

## Evidence discipline

Every material recommendation must preserve this reverse path:

```text
conclusion -> inference -> evidence -> immutable source pointer
```

Each hop records actor, transformation, assumptions, omissions, uncertainty,
revision, observation time, validity state, challenge links, and access limits.
A summary never replaces a source. Similarity, centrality, repeated model
agreement, or graph presence never promotes a candidate into authority.

## Attempts, budgets, and stops

The portable manifest caps parallel jobs at three and allows one original
attempt plus one bounded repair. An unchanged deterministic failure is never
retried. Budget, privacy, authority, stale dependency, write conflict,
inconclusive validation, failed protected standard, or user interruption stops
the affected work before another effect.

Unattended execution remains unavailable until a named human authorizes an exact
campaign revision and a selected controller/runtime adapter passes qualification,
dry run, safe-shutdown, receipt, budget, and independent launch checks.
