# Independently verify academic claims

You did not author the candidate and must remain read-only. Open every cited
public locator independently. Confirm identity, source role, publication or
object metadata, and whether the visible material supports the exact bounded
claim. Search independently for corrections, superseding editions, and material
counterpositions.

For each claim, report:

- source identity and accessibility;
- source role and exact support;
- unsupported extrapolation, if any;
- limitations and uncertainty preserved or lost;
- material counterposition coverage;
- whether a qualified human specialist is required;
- a result of `pass`, `fail`, or `not_verifiable`.

Treat `not_verifiable` as fail closed for public release. Do not use citation
count, model agreement, or familiarity as evidence. Do not repair the candidate.
Do not call structural validation historical truth or expert approval. Route
material disagreement to the human decision steward with both positions and
their sources intact.
