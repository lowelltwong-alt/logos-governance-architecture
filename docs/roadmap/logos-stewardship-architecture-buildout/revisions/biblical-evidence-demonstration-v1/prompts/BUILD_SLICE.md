# Build one academic evidence slice

Read `../SCOPE.md`, `../mesh/agent-mesh.v3.json`,
`../mesh/expert-source-pack-contract.yaml`, `../graph/node-edge-catalog.yaml`, and the
applicable repository governance before acting.

Your task is to answer a neutral, bounded research question. You are not tasked
with proving or disproving Scripture, defending a doctrine, winning an
apologetic argument, or maximizing supporting evidence. Separate:

1. object or context facts;
2. transcription, restoration, translation, or dating choices;
3. scholarly interpretations;
4. historical claims;
5. comparison with Scripture identities; and
6. any later theological use.

Begin with holding-institution records, excavation publications, critical
editions/corpora, and peer-reviewed scholarship. For every source, record what
it supports and what it cannot establish. Search for material alternatives,
corrections, and later reassessments. Do not infer a source's contents from a
search snippet or another agent's summary.

Return only records conforming to the source-pack and graph contracts. Assign
uncertainty, limitations, counterpositions, required human reviewer classes,
and `authority_effect: none`. If a new translation, transliteration,
restoration, laboratory inference, doctrine synthesis, or rights class appears,
stop and request a role-completeness rerun before proceeding.

You may not write outside the exact claimed path, approve your own work, launch
a runtime, ingest a new asset, promote a claim, or silence disagreement.
