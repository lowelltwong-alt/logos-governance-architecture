# Scope and authority boundary

## In scope

- One flagship claim-mediated archaeology comparison: Sennacherib and Lachish.
- Two compact evidence-and-disagreement cases: Tel Dan and the Pilate inscription.
- Two anti-overclaim controls: Jericho chronology and Hittite reception rhetoric.
- One exact licensed manuscript-side image: Cologne P66, TM 61627, verso.
- Four non-contiguous John 19 coverage segments cataloged for the combined recto-and-verso object record, not assigned to the verso image by this packet, plus public-domain English display text.
- Honest intersections with an immutable M7 public pointer and finer local candidate observations.
- An explicit M8-pending and convergence-not-started state.
- Typed nodes, edges, source roles, uncertainty, counterpositions, rights, reviews, and human gates.
- Deterministic validators, adversarial fixtures, specialist-mesh receipts, and public navigation.

## Out of scope

- Reading, OCRing, transcribing, or translating P66 from the photograph.
- Claiming that the exact verso JPEG contains any particular catalog segment until a qualified side-specific source or review establishes that mapping.
- Selecting a preferred textual reading or claiming P66 is the original text.
- Treating English wording as licensed by the image.
- Completing or converging M7 and M8.
- Declaring an archaeological theory settled or using an artifact to prove Scripture.
- Promoting historical context into canon, exegesis, Christian doctrine, or apologetic certainty.
- Activating a graph database, retrieval service, agent runtime, or autonomous research campaign.
- Ingesting any other source asset.

## Public claim rule

Every visible historical statement must identify its source role, locator,
bounded claim, limitations, uncertainty, counterposition search, and review
status. “No evidence” additionally requires a defined corpus, survey boundary,
date range, and retrieval date.

## Human review floors

| Claim class | Minimum human review before promotion beyond this demonstration |
|---|---|
| Assyrian inscription or royal-rhetoric synthesis | Assyriologist |
| Lachish excavation chronology and event linkage | Iron Age Levant archaeologist |
| Passage-to-campaign historical synthesis | Hebrew Bible historian |
| Tel Dan reading or reconstruction | Northwest Semitic epigrapher |
| Pilate restoration or Latin interpretation | Latin epigrapher or Roman historian |
| P66 transcription or textual inference | New Testament papyrologist/textual critic |
| Translation judgment | Qualified Greek/translation reviewer |
| Doctrine or theological use | Separately authorized qualified theological review |

These are reviewer-class requirements, not claims that such reviewers have
approved this packet.
