# Scope

## In scope

- additive specification documents;
- five draft JSON Schemas and their schema-registry entries;
- one empty, non-authorizing production domain-pack registry;
- synthetic positive and adversarial fixtures;
- an offline deterministic validator and focused tests;
- cross-links and governance dependency-map coverage;
- task-local mesh, standards, decision-gate, test, digest, and review receipts.

## Out of scope

- runtime launch, agent activation, provider binding, or external calls;
- source ingestion, source quotation, embeddings, or corpus construction;
- real academic, historical, archaeological, philosophical, scientific,
  theological, exegetical, or doctrinal assertions;
- approval of a domain pack or qualified reviewer;
- changes to Scripture, doctrine, canonicity, tradition policy, or authority;
- cross-repository writes, publication, push, pull request, merge, cleanup,
  reset, rebase, force operation, or deletion.

## Stop conditions

Stop on preflight failure, claim drift, unrelated conflict, kernel weakening,
authority leakage, ambiguous rights, an unrepresented consumer, a real source or
doctrine payload, or any unresolved human decision needed for the next action.
