# Whole-Doctrine Expert Mesh Revision V2

This revision specifies how a future governed campaign can select, qualify,
compose, check, replace, and retire task-local doctrine research roles. It also
specifies a **Doctrine Mesh Completeness Auditor** that must run before work,
at every material midflight trigger, and after work.

This directory is a saved specification and test harness. It is not a running
controller, a source corpus, a doctrinal conclusion, an approval, or permission
to launch research. No file in this revision changes Scripture, doctrine,
canonicity, a tradition's self-description, or another repository.

## What this revision adds

- a provider-neutral, depth-one expert mesh with workers, source checkers,
  entailment checkers, counterevidence challengers, alignment auditors, and
  human escalation;
- a bounded role factory that can automatically create **task-local candidate
  roles** only inside a previously authorized envelope;
- expert classifications spanning Scripture and original languages, ancient
  Jewish worlds, archaeology and ancient contexts, philosophy, Christian
  doctrine and reception, confessional traditions, and verification methods;
- an ExpertPack contract that supplies exact source manifests, non-evidentiary
  knowledge guides, qualification fixtures, and narrow scope cards;
- strict repository and graph boundaries that keep canonical Scripture,
  Christian doctrine, Jewish historical context, and external ancient context
  distinct;
- deterministic proof that required completeness-audit events were observed
  exactly once against the right inputs, plus independent review of whether the
  auditor's expert judgments were substantively adequate;
- neutral internal prompts that investigate competing hypotheses and forbid
  "prove this conclusion" assignments;
- a decision/risk contract that makes high-risk disagreements visible to Lowell
  without flooding him with routine validation failures;
- a red-team, premortem, repair ledger, and frozen Fable review packet.

## Determinism boundary

The validator can prove that this saved design-time audit bundle gives every
required audit event one fresh, schema-valid, hash-bound receipt in the correct
order, and it blocks this artifact's freeze when that bundle fails. It cannot
constrain a future work phase or prove that an agent identified every useful
expert. Future phase enforcement requires a separately authorized and qualified
runtime. Substantive coverage also requires a distinct read-only reviewer,
counterexamples, explicit omissions, and human escalation for high-risk doctrine
or authority questions.

## Automatic operation boundary

The future factory may route already-authorized low- or medium-risk candidate
research without routine human selection. It may not create recursive workers,
promote a role globally, change its own instructions, acquire paid or restricted
sources, decide doctrine, establish source authority, write another repository,
or activate a runtime. Those remain separately gated. Generated roles expire at
the end of the work unit unless a human approves a separately evaluated reusable
capability.

## What is deliberately not built

The source catalog and 27 ExpertPack blueprints are build specifications, not
knowledge bases or expert credentials. No source was browsed, ingested,
licensed, or qualified. No controller, lease service, budget ledger, consumer
validator, staleness engine, human-authentication adapter, cross-repository
writer, or substantive doctrine worker exists. Their schemas and contracts are
dormant interfaces. A green artifact validator must never be cited as runtime,
source, rights, human-authority, or doctrinal qualification.
