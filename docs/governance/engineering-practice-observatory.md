# Engineering Practice Observatory

## Core Question

> Would a leading engineering organization recognize this approach, what current
> primary evidence supports it, and does it fit Logos?

Never replace that question with "How do we imitate the largest company?" A
practice observed at scale may be expensive, organization-specific, immature, or
unsafe for a solo-maintainer theological knowledge system.

## Routine

| Trigger | Frequency | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Pull request or relevant architecture change | Near real time | Deterministic topic and due-date report |
| Codex research automation | Monday 09:00 America/New_York | Primary-source freshness research and recommendation queue |
| GitHub scheduled watch | Monday 09:17 America/New_York | Redundant due check and visible overdue issue |
| Deep synthesis | When monthly due dates mature | Multi-source fit and tradeoff review |
| Library gardening | Quarterly | Supersession, broken-link, duplicate, and beginner-clarity audit |
| Manual dispatch | Any time | Recovery when schedules fail or the owner requests review |

The GitHub schedule runs at minute 17 because GitHub documents greater delay near
the start of the hour. Scheduled execution is not proof of completed research.
Only a dated source verification and recommendation review clears a due item.

## Escalation

The cheap sentinel handles structured dates and changed paths. A research agent
activates only for due, novel, disputed, or high-leverage work. Independent review
and red-team activate for governance, architecture, authority, security, source
rights, costly automation, or practices that could change theological safeguards.

## Recommendation Contract

Every recommendation must preserve:

- the exact primary source and its organizational context;
- alternatives and "do not use when" conditions;
- Logos fit across solo maintenance, theology safety, auditability, cost,
  complexity, and future scale;
- status: adopt, pilot, watch, reject, owner decision required, or superseded;
- review date and expiration;
- owner authority over adoption.
