# Capability-First Routing Standard

The machine-readable source of truth is
[`governance/registry/agent_routing_vocabulary.yaml`](../../../governance/registry/agent_routing_vocabulary.yaml).

## Rule

Route a work contract by required capability, domain qualification, authority
ceiling, independence, and effort band. Among adapters that meet every hard
requirement, select the lowest expected total cost. Expected retries, defect
risk, review burden, and required research access are part of total cost.

Unknown qualification is not qualification. A current alias, provider, model
name, or effort label is an adapter fact, never evidence of scholarly or
theological competence.

## Economic Lanes

`W0_mechanical` and `W1_bounded_engineering` may use the cheapest qualified
deterministic worker when their inputs, writable paths, acceptance tests, and
stop conditions are bounded. They remain subject to their written contract.

`W2_specialist_research` requires documented domain qualification and an
independent review route for Greek, Hebrew/Aramaic, textual criticism/codices,
archaeology/history, theology/hermeneutics, and ontology/cross-repo
architecture. Its output remains evidence, candidate material, or review
preparation as the work contract permits.

`W3_cross_domain_architecture` is for hard cross-disciplinary design and must
use the highest needed qualified effort plus the independence named by the work
contract. It does not create theological authority.

## Human Gates

No adapter, agent, worker, reviewer, qualification fixture, or orchestration
result can satisfy `A3_human_reserved`. AI may prepare observations,
candidates, and decision packets only within its stated ceiling. Owner and
human gates remain outside the routing system.

## Non-Runtime Boundary

This standard does not create runtime agents, schedules, provider mappings,
durable role assignments, source imports, doctrine records, Scripture/chunk
outputs, or graph/retrieval/vector truth. AGENT-W7 qualification evidence is a
later, separately audited step; a missing result leaves an adapter unqualified.
